# Occupancy Guidelines

## Retail
Retail occupancies are generally moderate property risks when combustible storage is controlled and fire protection is adequate.

## Restaurant
Restaurants may have elevated fire exposure because of cooking operations, grease, heat-producing equipment, and combustible contents. Kitchen-fire history is a referral signal.

## Warehouse and manufacturing
Warehouses and manufacturing risks require attention to storage type, combustible loading, processes, and protection systems. Hazardous storage or high-hazard processes warrant enhanced review.
