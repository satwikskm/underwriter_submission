# Underwriting Decision Framework

## Auto quote
A clean, well-documented submission with low modeled risk and no material referral triggers may be eligible for automated quotation subject to applicable authority.

## Refer
Refer when risk indicators are mixed, when guideline evidence requires judgment, or when claims, protection, occupancy, or TIV create material uncertainty.

## Decline candidate
A decline candidate has severe or clearly out-of-appetite characteristics. The final decision remains subject to authorized underwriting rules and human review.
