# Commercial Property Guidelines

## Eligibility and TIV
Commercial property risks should have a clearly documented occupancy, construction type, protection features, and total insured value (TIV). Submissions with material missing information require underwriter review before binding.

## Referral thresholds
Refer when the risk has repeated material losses, unusual operations, inadequate protection, or characteristics outside the preferred appetite. A high TIV alone is not a decline reason, but it increases the importance of documented controls and loss history.

## Preferred characteristics
Preferred risks generally have noncombustible or fire-resistive construction, active sprinkler protection where appropriate, stable operations, and limited recent loss activity.
