# Claims Guidelines

## Loss history
Claims frequency and severity should be reviewed over the available policy years. A clean loss history is favorable, while repeated or severe claims increase referral likelihood.

## Loss ratio
Loss ratio is a useful quantitative signal but should not be interpreted in isolation. The underwriter should consider exposure growth, one-time events, open claims, and remediation.

## Documentation
Material claims should have a documented cause of loss and status. Missing or inconsistent claims information should be flagged for review.
