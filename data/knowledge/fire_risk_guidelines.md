# Fire Risk Guidelines

## Protection
Sprinkler protection is a favorable characteristic for property risks. Lack of sprinklers in an occupancy with meaningful combustible loading should trigger additional review.

## Prior fire losses
Multiple recent fire-related claims should be treated as a referral signal. The underwriter should review cause, severity, remediation, and whether controls were changed after each loss.

## Construction
Frame construction generally carries more fire exposure than masonry noncombustible or fire-resistive construction. Construction should be considered together with occupancy and protection.
